# Simple Interest Calculator

A basic calculator that calculates simple interest based on the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

## Inputs

* Principal amount
* Rate of interest
* Time period

## Output

The calculator displays the calculated simple interest based on the values provided by the user.

## How It Works

The calculator accepts the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period as inputs. It then applies the simple interest formula and displays the calculated result.
